# First-join Voxy mesh admission

Capture 20261027-213440-7f017f, client build2026102711: between 5 and 25 seconds after connection, samples showed all 11 dedicated Voxy workers executing section mesh generation while EMI reloaded for about 19.1 seconds. Samples do not prove simultaneous occupation or quantify causation. The first-join frame stream and shader work also show stalls; this targets CPU contention, not all stutter.

The exact installed Voxy port.9 RenderGenerationService creates a named service in Voxy's own ServiceManager. During the already-scoped EMI reload only, gate this service to at most four concurrent mesh jobs. Its native queue, service task permits, execution order, model requests, section data, LOD levels, uploads, and all other services remain intact. The scheduler limiter is nonblocking; a concurrent race between checking the limit and actually starting a job is closed by an atomic reservation around the original Service.runJob. A failed reservation leaves the native permit and queue untouched. Jobs already running before the scope began finish; no additional job enters above the cap. On normal completion, error or cancellation the reservation is released, and when EMI finishes native concurrency resumes. The cap is a provisional responsiveness/throughput tradeoff: initial distant LOD can become detailed later.

The exact Voxy classes plus EMI reload-worker version/hashes are guarded; mismatch disables both mixins. Diagnostic opt-out: `-Dprominence.emiHome.voxyMeshAdmission=false`. A cumulative blocker/race counter is logged on scope completion; it counts limiter checks, not jobs or time saved. No setting, server, protocol, GPU upload stream, frame order or LOD quality is changed.

Headless verification transforms the actual installed Voxy service and RenderGenerationService, checks ASM, and runs the transformed native Service with actual Voxy ServiceManager and UnifiedServiceThreadPool: 24 mesh/24 unrelated jobs, cap, restoration, shutdown while capped, scheduler progress and no lost jobs. Existing Voxy decode, EMI, updater and other regression suites pass. These use bounded synthetic jobs and do not start the game, render LODs, establish an in-game speedup or prove timing under all mod interactions. Real play and a comparable first-join frame/JFR trace are required; if LOD completion becomes excessively slow or stalls, disable/revert the change and investigate.
